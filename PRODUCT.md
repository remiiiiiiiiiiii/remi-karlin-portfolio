# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

A mix of film producers, art directors, brand clients, and agencies — people actively evaluating whether to work with Remi or wanting to understand his creative direction. They arrive with a professional eye. Their job: quickly read his aesthetic sensibility, decide if it matches what they need, and explore further if it does.

## Product Purpose

An art director's portfolio, with the films and photographs he shoots, edits and grades. The site exists to communicate creative identity before a single word is read — the design is itself a demonstration of taste. Success means a visitor understands Remi's vibe within seconds, feels compelled to explore, and leaves impressed.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, precise, understated.

## Anti-references

None specified. Avoid anything that feels busy, decorative for its own sake, or tries too hard to signal creativity through visual noise. The work speaks — the site should get out of its way.

## Design Principles

1. **The design is the work.** Every spacing decision, transition, and typographic choice is a creative statement. Nothing is accidental.
2. **Restraint is the loudest signal.** Black, white, one accent. Silence is part of the composition.
3. **Show, don't explain.** Let the films and images carry the meaning. Copy is sparse, purposeful, and never redundant.
4. **Earn every moment of attention.** Animations and reveals should feel inevitable, not decorative. If it doesn't add weight, remove it.
5. **Coherence across all surfaces.** From selected works to brand identity projects, every page should feel like it belongs to the same director's cut.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
